Overview

Frøya Organics needed more than a visual refresh — the brief was to work on ecommerce strategy and product-page optimisation. That meant treating the storefront as a system to test and iterate on, not a fixed layout to polish once.

Approach

  • User journey analysis

    Mapped where people moved through the site and where that flow broke down.

  • Experimentation

    Tested variations against real traffic, so every change to the product page and journey was backed by data rather than opinion.

  • Drop-off identification

    Looked specifically at where users were leaving the purchase journey.

  • Iteration

    Fed what the tests showed back into the product-page and journey design.

Impact

  • Up to +18% sales

    Sales grew by up to 18% over a 3-month period.

  • +11% entries

    11% more entries over a 4-month period compared with the previous year.

  • Up to 12% lower cart abandonment

    Cart abandonment fell by up to 12% over a 4-month period as the purchase journey was simplified.

  • Fewer steps

    Unnecessary steps between product discovery and purchase were removed.
