Overview

Frøya Organics needed more than a visual refresh — the brief was to work on ecommerce strategy and product-page optimisation with the goal of improving sales. That meant treating the storefront as a system to test and iterate on, not a fixed layout to polish once.

Approach

  • User journey analysisMapped where people moved through the site and where that flow broke down.
  • A/B testingTested variations against real traffic rather than deciding by opinion.
  • Drop-off identificationLooked specifically at where users were leaving the purchase journey.
  • IterationFed what the tests showed back into the product-page and journey design.

Impact

+18%

Sales increased.

+11%

Users spent 11% more time engaging with product pages.

↓

Cart abandonment — the purchase journey was simplified to reduce drop-off.

Fewer steps

Reduced unnecessary steps between product discovery and purchase.
