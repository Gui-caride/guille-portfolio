About me

Questions before solutions.

I am a UX/UI Designer with 6 years of experience working across different clients, industries and types of digital products.

Working in an agency environment has given me a broad perspective across ecommerce, product experiences, design systems and large-scale digital ecosystems. I am now interested in moving further into Product Design, where I can be involved throughout the process — from understanding the problem and asking the right questions, through UX and UI, collaboration with developers and implementation.

Questions before solutions.

Curious by default

I like understanding how things work, asking questions and looking at problems from different angles.

I tend to look beyond the interface itself — what is the user trying to do, where is the complexity coming from, and what could be made simpler?

I am organised in my process, but curious enough to challenge it when something does not make sense.

What I bring

STRATEGY

I like understanding the problem before jumping into the solution.

UX

I think through the full user journey and look for ways to make complex experiences simpler.

UI

I care about visual hierarchy, interaction and the details that make an experience feel coherent.

SYSTEMS

I enjoy creating structure that can scale, from design systems to reusable patterns.

COLLABORATION

I like working closely with different disciplines and understanding how design becomes a real product.

LEARNING

I learn quickly and enjoy getting into unfamiliar problems, industries and tools.
