ExxonMobil Design System

A standardized set of reusable components, guidelines and patterns for ExxonMobil's internal digital platforms — built for consistency, accessibility and efficiency across applications used by teams in multiple regions.

Overview

I was part of the team migrating ExxonMobil's design tooling from Adobe XD to Figma, which meant streamlining collaboration between designers and developers from the ground up. To make the migration actually stick, I built a comprehensive guide that unified workflows and served as a single source of truth for design standards.

Working inside Agile squads, I partnered directly with developers, product owners and stakeholders to validate feasibility and keep the system consistent. I also ran user and stakeholder interviews, plus usability testing, to catch gaps and refine components against real-world use.

The problem

ExxonMobil ran on multiple internal digital platforms, but with no unified design system behind them — which meant inconsistencies, duplicated design and engineering effort, and accessibility gaps. The design team needed something scalable: a system that could improve collaboration, hold visual consistency, and streamline workflows across global teams.

Process

01

Benchmark

Direct competitors

Reviewed whether other global oil & gas companies had public or internal design guidelines, with an eye on consistency, accessibility and tone.

Best-in-class systems

Studied mature systems like IBM Carbon, Google Material, Atlassian and Microsoft Fluent for documentation style, governance models, accessibility standards and component scalability.

Approach comparison

Weighed rigid, brand-driven consistency against systems that leave room for local or product-specific adaptation.

02

Execution

Audit & migration

Reviewed the existing Adobe XD components, flagged inconsistencies, and rebuilt them as scalable, reusable Figma components backed by design tokens for color, grid and typography.

Collaboration & documentation

Wrote a comprehensive guide covering usage, accessibility rules and best practices, so designers and developers were finally working from a shared language.

Validation

Ran usability testing and stakeholder interviews to validate components, and worked with developers throughout to keep everything feasible and aligned with the code libraries.

03

Insights

The work created a shared foundation for collaboration, consistency and accessibility across ExxonMobil's internal applications.

Document the "what" and the "how"

Designers, developers and stakeholders only stay aligned when documentation covers both what a component is and how to actually use it.

Tokens over fixed values

Figma variables for color, typography and spacing gave the system the flexibility to iterate fast without breaking consistency.

Responsive by default

Every component needed to hold up across screen sizes and devices without a separate design pass each time.
